❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

I can't log in. What should I do?

Make sure you have entered the correct Login URI in your viewer. Also check your credentials (first name, last name, and password).

How do I create my own region?

Contact the grid administrators for information about hosting your own regions.

I forgot my password. What now?

Visit our password recovery page and follow the instructions.

Is it free to use?

Using the grid is free. For details about premium features or region hosting, please contact the administrators.
